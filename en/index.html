How to use this notebook

01 Read little, experiment a lot

Each section sums up the essentials of the lecture notes in a few boxes, then puts an apparatus in your hands. Change a parameter, predict the result, then run the experiment.

02 Check your calculations

The exercises accept answers such as 1/sqrt(2) or pi/3. Your progress stays in your browser: no account, no data sent anywhere.

03 Critique the analogies

Ndolé, Eshu’s crossroads, the spinning coin: the course’s analogies help you get started, but each one breaks down somewhere. The notebook asks you where.

04 Work without a connection

After a first visit, the pages you have opened remain available offline. On a phone, “Add to Home screen” installs the notebook like an app.
